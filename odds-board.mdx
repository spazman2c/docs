Open [Odds board](https://oddswire.io/app/odds) when the add-on is available to your account. Choose the bookmakers and sports you want to view, then find a fixture in each panel.

## Arrange your board

Use the toolbar to choose bookmakers, sports, layout and card size. Only one toolbar menu opens at a time. Online bookmakers appear first; offline options are greyed out and cannot be selected. A selected bookmaker can also become unavailable while you are viewing it.

Use **Saved fixtures** to return to matches you saved earlier. Saving a fixture stores its reference, not a guaranteed current price. See [Fixtures and saved matches](/fixtures).

## Read a price

Each panel keeps the bookmaker, fixture, market and selection together. Check the market period, line and price unit before comparing values. A probability from a prediction market is not decimal odds.

Prices update while the board is active, subject to source availability. Pausing updates does not stop prices ageing. Stale, suspended or unavailable prices are withheld; a page refresh does not make an old source price current. **Status unavailable** means the latest monitoring check could not confirm collection, rather than proving the bookmaker is offline.

## Compare compatible markets

Compare the same outcome, period, line and settlement terms. Similar names do not guarantee that two books handle postponements, restarts or payouts the same way. Read the displayed comparison warnings.

A higher price alone does not establish positive expected value or arbitrage. Fair-value displays require eligible references and may show an unavailable reason. See [fair values](/data#fair-price) and [+EV finder](/ev-finder).

If you cannot select a bookmaker, check [Coverage](https://oddswire.io/app/coverage) and [service status](https://oddswire.io/status). An active plan does not keep an offline feed running.
