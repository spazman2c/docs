Restricted source-native REST contract. Requires its explicit native feature, namespace and source review. Kambi retained snapshots preserve original capture times; live mode additionally requires the founder live review and current controls. No general customer availability or canonical player/settlement mapping is implied. A nonempty eligible native data page costs one credit. Empty native fixture pages and native odds pages without eligible records are free; inspect Usage for final settlement. Errors and 304 are free. See [Current availability](/availability).
