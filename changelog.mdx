## 16 November 2026

- Offline bookmaker options are greyed out; online bookmakers appear first in supported selectors.
- Odds workspace toolbar menus are smaller, animate open and closed, and open one at a time.
- Workspace [Support](/support) opens Intercom for new tickets and follow-ups. Earlier requests remain available separately.
- Historical odds are no longer listed as a public product in the documentation.

## 15 November 2026

- [Fixtures and Saved fixtures](/fixtures) let you browse matches, save references and explicitly pull their odds.
- **In-play** now distinguishes after-kickoff markets from continuously updated pregame prices. Pregame updates do not require the In-play option.
- Native sports and competition discovery, market-family filters and reviewed shared fixture UUIDs help narrow requests. See [Datasets](/datasets).
- Eligible fair-value reads support multiplicative, power and Shin methods. Independent-reference requirements still apply to each market.
- MCP includes comparison and settlement checks, with original price age and explicit missing-reference reasons.

## 13 November 2026

- Guides cover [plan customization](/plans), [Usage](/usage), [Odds board](/odds-board), [Terminal](/terminal), [+EV finder](/ev-finder), [Bet tracker](/bet-tracker), [Alerts](/alerts) and [Discord posts](/discord).
- Bet tracker supports manual records, CSV preview/import, results, revision history, delete/restore and exports.

## 12 November 2026

- [MCP](/mcp-client) supports Oddswire sign-in for compatible OAuth clients and API-key authentication for supported hosts.
- [WebSocket documentation](/websocket) covers server connections, browser tickets and native snapshot delivery.

## 11 November 2026

- Added shorter dataset aliases, opt-in `view=standard`, [participants](/reference/listParticipants) and [fixture details](/reference/getFixture).
- HTTPS quick starts use standard tools and header credentials.

## Compatibility

The REST API uses `/v1`. Preserve original identifiers and allow for added optional fields and new error or missing-coverage codes. Check [availability](/availability) and your account coverage before depending on a feature.
