title: "Fixtures and saved matches"
description: "Find matches, save references and request their odds when you need them."
---

Open [Fixtures](https://oddswire.io/app/fixtures) to browse matches from the bookmakers and sports available to your account.

## Find a match

1. Choose a bookmaker and sport, and any available phase or market options.
2. Load a page of fixtures. Each successful fixture page uses one data credit, including an eligible empty page.
3. Search the results already loaded. Open a fixture's details to see its competition, kickoff, source identity and available metadata.
4. Request the next page explicitly if more results are available.

Opening the page, searching loaded results and copying or exporting those results do not make another paid data request. Results are snapshots; loading them does not start continuous polling.

## Save a fixture for later

Save the exact fixture reference, then open [Saved fixtures](https://oddswire.io/app/fixtures/saved) when you want to return to it. Saved references belong to you within the workspace. Saving or removing a reference is free and does not store a price or preserve access to the bookmaker.

From Saved fixtures, choose **Pull odds** to request prices for that match. Each successful odds page follows the normal data-credit rules. More pages require another explicit request. Check [Usage](/usage) if a request times out before trying again.

## Understand the results

Kickoffs are shown in UTC. Missing scores, venue details, player IDs or badges are left unavailable rather than guessed. A match with the same name at another bookmaker can have a different source ID; keep the bookmaker and fixture identity together.

A saved match can become unavailable if it ends, the source stops updating or your permissions expire. You can still remove its saved reference. Exports describe the delivered results and whether further pages remain; they do not automatically fetch the remaining pages.

For an automatically refreshing view, use the [Odds board](/odds-board).
