Open [Odds board](https://oddswire.io/app/odds) from your workspace when the add-on is available. Select the books, sport, competition and market offered within your account's coverage.

## Read the board

Rows identify the fixture, market and selection. Prices retain their bookmaker and region. Check the displayed state and confirmation age before using a price. A recent page refresh does not make an old source price current.

Stale, suspended, missing or inaccessible prices are withheld from active comparisons. If a request fails, use the displayed retry control; do not treat an earlier value as a current quote.

Compare only the same market, period, line and settlement terms. Home/draw/away prices with different postponement or restart rules can be shown for research without being interchangeable. Read any comparison warning. A high price alone does not establish a positive expected value or arbitrage opportunity.

## Filters and updates

Use the available fixture pages and filters to narrow the view. Coverage can differ by bookmaker, region and market. The visible board refreshes automatically; confirm the current state after returning to a tab or changing scope.

If the page reports unavailable access, review [your plan](/plans) and [Coverage](https://oddswire.io/app/coverage). A source listed in the public directory is not necessarily enabled on your account.

For the dedicated interface, see [Terminal](/terminal). For reference-based analysis, see [+EV finder](/ev-finder). Historical charts require separate history access and are not included merely because current odds are available.
