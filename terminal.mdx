Open [Terminal](https://oddswire.io/terminal) when it is available to your account. Terminal requires its own active add-on and Terminal data permissions; an API key or API allocation does not unlock it.

## Set your scope

Choose your country/state preferences, available books, sport, competition and market. Preferences help filter your view; they do not change your location eligibility or data permissions. Settings are saved per member. If another session changes them, reload before saving again.

The board shows source-labelled prices, market terms, confirmation ages and missing coverage. Decimal odds and prediction-market probabilities use different units. Keep each quote's source and settlement context when comparing them.

## Comparisons and freshness

Only eligible, complete comparisons are shown. A stale, suspended or missing outcome can remove a comparison even while another book continues updating. Ordinary 1X2 comparison does not by itself establish identical postponement/restart terms. Read the displayed warning before drawing conclusions.

Prices expire according to the source's freshness policy. Pausing or returning to a tab does not freeze quote ages. An unavailable request cannot refresh the last price.

## Charts and fair values

Charts require separate historical coverage and permission. Where available, select the exact quote and a supported time window. Gaps and suspensions remain visible; missing history is not filled with current prices.

Fair values and [+EV](/ev-finder) require eligible, independent and settlement-compatible references. **Insufficient eligible reference** means no supported calculation is available for that selection. It is not a zero edge or a recommendation.

Terminal views do not consume REST data credits. Access and usage limits still apply. See [Availability](/availability), [data conventions](/data) and the [Odds board guide](/odds-board).
