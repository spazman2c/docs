## Try current fixtures and odds

**Try it is preconfigured for current data.** Enter your API key, open [Find fixtures](/reference/listFixtures) and press **Send**. The current feed, BetRivers PA, scheduled status and standard response view are already filled in; the initial page is limited to three records. You can optionally filter by bookmaker, competition or start time.

Copy an `id` from the returned `data`, open [Get odds](/reference/listOdds), enter that ID as `fixture` and press **Send**. The same bookmaker, current feed, standard view and match-result market are preselected. Your key's source permissions and credit allocation still apply.

## Check price freshness

Current prices use `evidence.mode=current_canonical`. A usable price has `state=active`, a non-null `value` and `evidence.fresh_for_live_use=true`. `evidence.confirmed_at` preserves the original confirmation time. Delivery time does not refresh a source quote.

Stale, suspended or unavailable prices remain withheld. Inspect every `missing[]` entry. An empty result is valid when nothing in the requested scope is available.

## Fields and identity

- Fixtures include an exact string `id`, schedule, status, participants and evidence.
- Odds include fixture, source, market and selection IDs, line and unit, string-valued prices, state and evidence.
- Keep dataset and source identity with IDs. Matching numbers do not establish that two records describe the same match.
- `original` preserves the detailed source record. Do not convert decimal strings or large identifiers into floating-point numbers.
- Market family, period, line units and settlement terms must agree before comparing prices.

## Integration settings

The tester supplies `namespace=regional` and `view=standard` for the default current-data flow. Keep those values for both fixture discovery and odds requests. `books` optionally selects bookmaker/source IDs. A source unavailable to your key stays unavailable; changing a setting does not grant access.

If your integration has a separately configured feed, use the settings supplied for that integration and preserve its IDs. Contact [support](/support) if it is not listed in your access response.

## Fixtures and participants

[Fixture details](/reference/getFixture) retrieves one ID returned by discovery. [Teams and players](/reference/listParticipants) lists available participant evidence. Missing identity remains explicit; names alone do not establish a verified cross-bookmaker match.
