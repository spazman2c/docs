Every data request names a namespace. Canonical bulk odds also requires a `fixture` or `competition` filter. Discover fixtures and sources inside that namespace; never join different datasets merely because numeric IDs match. `books` contains **source/region IDs**, not brand slugs. Correlated brands in the same feed family do not become independent sharp references.

## Current data

The [fixture and odds tester](/endpoints) is preconfigured for the current feed and standard response view. Use a fixture ID returned by discovery. Check `state`, `value`, `evidence.fresh_for_live_use` and the original `evidence.confirmed_at` before using a price. Source, player, fee and settlement verification fields remain explicit.

## Quote identity and units

Preserve `quote_key`, native fixture/offer/outcome IDs, source/region, side and depth. More than one native offer may exist on the same canonical line. Reviewed grouping/orientation is explicit; source-local identity is not a reviewed match. Market family, period, quarter line and settlement terms must be compatible before comparison.

`decimal_odds`, native probabilities and sizes are decimal **strings**. A probability is not decimal odds. A team's NO contract is not the opposing 1X2 outcome. Fees, commissions, liquidity and settlement metadata remain source-specific. Unknown/nonzero exchange fees and push/split payouts are unsupported in the fair service.

## Freshness and missing coverage

`source_ts` is nullable. `seen_ts` is observation time. `confirmed_ts` records the last successful confirmation; unchanged successful observations refresh it without another price tick. `sent_ts` only records delivery and cannot refresh source freshness.

Read quote eligibility/state and age against `freshness_limit_seconds`; distinguish active, suspended, unavailable and stale states. Read **all** `missing[]` entries, including when `data` is nonempty. They identify uncollected, denied, stale, blocked, incompatible or insufficient coverage. A response is never proof that every planned bookmaker or market is covered.

The read-only API does not collect new prices. Free account coverage describes available scope and source freshness; it is not an uptime guarantee. Fixture discovery may return no data. Production coverage guarantees remain unverified.

## Fair price

`GET /v1/fair` requires fixture and settlement rule as well as namespace. Use discovered market metadata. The service checks the current eligible inputs, independent pricing groups and configured weights for each request. It exposes sources/method/filters and explicit insufficiency rather than serving an expired calculation.

The book-consensus policy requires **at least two eligible independent pricing groups**; a sharp book such as Pinnacle is not required. Correlated brands count once. Availability is evaluated for each request: a selected market can be insufficient even when another market has enough references. Methods are `multiplicative`, `power` and `shin`, and the result identifies the method and original input clocks. An insufficient result has no usable fair price. A 200 insufficient result still costs one credit. Technical source access does not confer customer display, derived-use or redistribution rights.
