---
title: "In-play availability"
description: "Check whether live markets are available before requesting them."
---

Customer in-play data is not currently available. The documented working data scope is pre-match; selecting a live option in a plan estimate does not enable a live feed.

Check account [Coverage](https://oddswire.io/app/coverage) and the source's `inplay.mode` before using a live filter. A live request also needs explicit in-play permission; WebSocket access alone does not provide it.

Fixture phase and price state are different. A suspension is not proof that a match started or ended, and reaching scheduled kickoff is not proof of a live source update. Scores, game clocks and results are not included by the odds endpoints.

Use [availability](/availability) for current feature status and [data conventions](/data) for freshness and missing coverage. Contact [support](mailto:admin@oddswire.io) with your required sport and market before building a live-data dependency.
