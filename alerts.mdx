Open [Alerts](https://oddswire.io/app/alerts) with an active Alerts add-on and permitted source coverage. Owners and admins manage rules. A saved plan alone does not enable alerts.

## Create a rule

1. Choose **Create alert** and select an available fixture and exact market outcome.
2. Choose a rule type: **Probability movement** or **Odds reach a threshold**.
3. Enter the threshold and direction, the allowed gap between observations and a cooldown.
4. Choose the offered delivery channels, review the rule and save.

A probability-movement threshold measures absolute implied-probability change in basis points. One basis point is 0.01 percentage points. Decimal odds moving from 2.00 to 4.00 change implied probability from 50% to 25%: −2,500 bps, rather than a 25% relative odds move.

A decimal-threshold rule watches the chosen crossing direction at the entered decimal price. It is a condition on the selected quote, not a fair-price or positive-EV signal.

## Channels and alert inbox

The browser inbox records alert events. Email can be selected where it is configured and available to your account; a saved email preference does not prove delivery. Inspect the event's channel status. Provider acceptance does not establish that a person received or read the message.

The workspace shows the latest 100 events with UTC timestamps and refreshes every 10 seconds. Review current state, event time and delivery status together. Browser rules do not imply a guaranteed desktop notification after the tab closes.

## Edit or stop a rule

Change the settings or pause/disable a rule in the workspace. A rule follows one exact native quote; a similarly named outcome from another book is not a substitute. Rules require valid observations and do not backfill old movements when created.

Stale, suspended, missing or changed evidence cannot trigger an eligible price alert. Missing access and a failed service request are different states: use the displayed retry control for a connection failure. Neither repeating a denied request nor adjusting a threshold grants source access.

For custom receiver integrations, see [Signed webhooks](/webhooks). For updating channel messages, see [Discord posts](/discord).
