Build your selection in [Customize plan](https://oddswire.io/app/plan). The builder has four steps: **Bookmakers**, **Sports**, **Add-ons** and **Requests**.

1. Choose the bookmakers and regions you need.
2. Choose sports and the supported data options you want to estimate.
3. Select optional features.
4. Choose request volume and review the monthly estimate.

## Optional add-ons

| Add-on | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Bet tracker](/bet-tracker) | Manual bet records, CSV import, results and exports |
| [WebSockets](/websocket) | Streaming access for approved data scopes |
| [Alerts](/alerts) | Rules for selected price movements and thresholds |
| [Discord posts](/discord) | Updating odds messages in your chosen Discord channel |
| [+EV finder](/ev-finder) | Expected-value analysis when eligible references are available |
| [Odds board](/odds-board) | Compare available bookmaker prices |
| [Terminal](/terminal) | A dedicated odds research interface |

These are separate selections. One add-on does not automatically include another. Feature access requires an active account-specific purchase as well as any required data permissions.

## How the estimate works

Bookmaker and sport prices use the current published catalogue. Applicable data-option and request-volume multipliers affect the data subtotal. Monthly add-on fees are added afterward, so increasing request volume does not multiply the add-on fees.

Prices can change as the live catalogue updates. The builder refreshes the selection and estimate; an unavailable catalogue means a current estimate cannot be shown. Use the [pricing page](https://oddswire.io/pricing) and builder for current amounts rather than an old screenshot or downloaded estimate.

Saving a draft or downloading it records your selection. It does not charge you, create a subscription, add credits or activate features. Catalogue listings and in-play/props pricing options do not guarantee those feeds are available for your account.

## Checkout and billing

Follow the billing state displayed in your workspace. If Checkout is unavailable, no purchase can be completed there. Public paid checkout is not currently generally available; contact [support](mailto:admin@oddswire.io) about access. Do not treat a saved plan or a payment return page as confirmation of activation.

For existing access, check [Coverage](https://oddswire.io/app/coverage), [Usage](https://oddswire.io/app/usage) and the plan shown in your workspace. If a billing action has an uncertain result, reopen the existing action rather than submitting a new purchase.
