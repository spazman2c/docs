Historical reads require separate `history` permission and source coverage for historical use. Exports additionally require `history_export` and export permission. Current odds, Terminal access and a selected plan do not automatically include history. Check [account coverage](https://oddswire.io/app/coverage) and [availability](/availability).

## Permanent native identity

Supply `namespace`, source/region `books`, and `native_fixture_id` (up to ten source-local canonical fixture IDs from mappings' `native_fixture_id`). These are distinct from upstream `source_fixture_id` strings and reviewed group IDs. History keeps original source/native offer/outcome/side/depth identities. Mapping corrections never rewrite a tick or imply that today's grouping was valid in the past.

## Ticks and coverage

`GET /v1/history/ticks` requires inclusive received-time `from` and exclusive `until` bounds, at most 31 days. Tied observations order by immutable ingestion sequence. Unchanged successful observations refresh the separate confirmation journal, not tick history. Preserve decimal/probability strings, nullable `source_ts`, original `seen_ts` and native units; replay never refreshes those clocks.

Every page includes a `history` manifest with the actual retained range, prospective confirmation range/gaps, unavailable or unverified evidence and explicit missing reasons. The free `/v1/history/coverage` control exposes retained evidence ranges, with a separate flag indicating whether selected historical observations were verified. A retained range is not continuous coverage or an audit of every historical sink row. Empty/legacy/missing periods are not backfilled or advertised as multi-year data.

A request supports at most 2,000 ticks or 500 native slots and uses normal 1–100 row pages. Narrow the selection on 422. Cursors bind account/key/grants, filters and the complete retained snapshot; a 409 needs an explicit restart. They never silently skip newly accepted work.

## As-of and lines

`GET /v1/history/snapshot` requires a nonfuture `as_of`. It selects the last recorded confirmation at or before that time per permanent native key, with capture sequence breaking ties. Original price observation time remains distinct from confirmation time. Missing, stale-at-as-of, suspended, unavailable and source-gap evidence remain explicit; no older active quote replaces a later suspension.

`GET /v1/history/lines` also requires an explicit `scheduled_start`. Opening means the first eligible **recorded** pre-start confirmation, not the bookmaker's genuine market opening. Closing uses the established scheduled-start policy: the last confirmation strictly before the requested start, with matching recorded kickoff/state, freshness and liquidity checks. A requested start not yet reached at `as_of` is `not_final`. Unknown legacy confirmations and incompatible/missing evidence do not produce a closing value.

All these results are historical evidence, not executable prices or a settlement/fee compatibility approval. Freshness limits are the current reviewed source policy used to evaluate the requested historical cutoff.

## Complete CSV and Parquet files

`GET /v1/history/export` uses `kind=ticks|snapshot|lines`, the corresponding time parameters, and `format=csv|parquet`. It exports the **whole bounded selection**, with no `limit` or `cursor`. Files are capped at 2 MiB; larger selections fail explicitly rather than truncating. A manifest row carries coverage/query metadata even for an empty selection. Parquet also embeds it in footer metadata.

Canonical decimals remain UTF-8 strings; IDs and nulls retain their meaning. Nested canonical/native fields live losslessly in `record_json`; arbitrary native text is not placed in spreadsheet formula cells. CSV empty decimal cells represent null; Parquet has nullable string columns. Parse IDs/sequences as integers or strings rather than through spreadsheet floating-point inference.

Read exports as bytes and inspect the content/disposition/digest headers. Do not follow credential redirects or automatically retry an uncertain metered download. One completed successful history page or complete file costs one included credit, even when empty. Coverage, errors and 304 remain free; overage stays off. Rights and current access are rechecked before data/304 and after a download reservation.
