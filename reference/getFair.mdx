Restricted fair-price contract. No eligible sharp reference is configured; an insufficient result must not be interpreted as a fair price. A completed successful data page costs one credit, including an empty or insufficient page. Errors and 304 are free; a client timeout can leave delivery uncertain. See [Current availability](/availability).
