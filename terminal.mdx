**Availability:** the Terminal is a development preview, not a deployed customer product. The ordinary customer workspace login is live, but does not unlock Terminal. Customer rollout and source display/chart/fair rights remain pending. See [Current availability](/availability).

A session uses the **Terminal** product grant under the shared account. API keys,
API plans and beta enrollment do not unlock it. Terminal views consume no REST
credits, including at zero quota; normal abuse limits still apply. Country/state
and my-books preferences do not prove location or grant source rights.

Choose country/state, data workspace, your granted source/region books, sport,
competition, market/line and phase. Saves are per member; conflicting revisions
require reloading settings. Real working collectors remain pre-match-only; live
needs a separate product grant and validated source support. Read [in-play scope](/inplay).

Each native price retains its source, region, offer, outcome, side and depth.
Source-local rows and different settlements stay separate. Best gross labels
compare matching sportsbook scopes with exact decimal prices. Prediction-market
probabilities remain explicitly labeled and separate. Fair references require
reviewed grouping, current eligible inputs, Terminal fair access and independent
source-purpose approval; insufficient or unavailable references stay explicit.

Quote ages use successful confirmation time. Prices disappear when stale,
suspended or unavailable; pausing does not freeze their ages. A visible tab polls
at ten-second intervals, clearing errors and access changes. Source status and
missing reasons distinguish blocked rights, unsupported coverage and outages.

A fixture drawer reads one exact native quote from verified retained history,
with 1/6/24-hour windows and a 500-point bound. Charts preserve A→B→A, recorded
suspensions and freshness gaps. Exact points, ingest IDs and coverage metadata
remain available; no missing history is replaced with demo prices. See [history semantics](/history).

Session-only routes are [context](/reference/terminalContext),
[preferences](/reference/terminalPreferences), [board](/reference/terminalBoard)
and [chart](/reference/terminalChart). They are separate from API-key data routes.
The board is bounded to ten fixtures and 500 native cells per page; pagination
reads current snapshots. Larger virtualized streaming, opportunities, stakes,
alerts, tracking and subscriptions remain later Terminal tasks.
